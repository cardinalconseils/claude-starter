- Published: 2026-09-22

- Summary: CVE-2026-94127, a heap-based buffer overflow (CWE-122) in F5 BIG-IP when an APM

  active exploitation.

- Action: Identify BIG-IP systems with both an APM access policy and an OAuth profile
  configured; apply F5's fixed hotfix releases immediately
- URL: https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/alerts-advisories/f5-security-advisory-av26-949
