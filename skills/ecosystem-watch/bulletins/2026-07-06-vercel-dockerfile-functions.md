## What Changed
Announced: Vercel Functions now support deploying HTTP servers from a Dockerfile or Containerfile using OCI-compatible images on Fluid compute. Add a Dockerfile.vercel file to your project; Vercel builds, stores, deploys, and autoscales the image. Full details at https://vercel.com/changelog/bring-your-dockerfile-to-vercel-functions
