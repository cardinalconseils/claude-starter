## What Changed
Announced: The data behind Vercel's AI Gateway leaderboards is now open under the CC BY 4.0 license. It can be downloaded or queried through the leaderboard-export API endpoint, and any chart can be rendered as a shareable image. Available as of July 14, 2026.
